# Demo Training Samples

Synthetic demo set: 9 PDFs, 10 PNG images, and 9 XLSX workbooks. Types: RFQ, PO, PR, Design Document, BOM, Quotation, Invoice, Mixed and Unknown.

The mixed PDF has two pages, the mixed workbook has two sheets, and its PNGs are separate page images. Select one format per sample unless demonstrating duplicate/format handling. Upload only document files, not this README or answer-key.json.

The PNGs are clean rendered pages for OCR demos, not camera photos. XLSX files preserve reference/part identifiers as text and dates as date cells; no macros or external links. Stated figures are sample source data, not calculations.

These examples are not approved training records and may require correction in the current classifier/model. Company approval in the legacy Training screen requires matching lookup entries; your lookup sheet has not been changed.

Expected types/references are in answer-key.json.
